---
title: Training needs assessment
summary: Training budget is being spent, but you cannot show which skills gaps it closed — or whether it closed any.
detail: We map required competencies against current capability, identify where development actually moves performance, and give L&D a measurement plan tied to those gaps.
order: 2
---

Needs assessment is where L&D measurement starts. Without it, program evaluation has nothing to evaluate against. We treat the assessment itself as a research product: defined constructs, evidence of reliability, and a clear link to the business problem.
