GEMS Education operates a large international school network with high-volume teacher hiring and material turnover. The engagement produced a selection instrument built and documented to professional testing standards — not a generic interview guide.

The methodological work is the product. Every phase left an audit trail a talent or legal reviewer can follow: how constructs were defined, how items performed, how adverse impact was examined, and how cut scores were justified.
