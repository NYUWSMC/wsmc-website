## Time commitment

Up to eight hours per week during the academic year. That includes project delivery, internal training and client meetings.

## Process

1. **Apply** — open to first-year students in the NYU I-O Psychology master’s program.
2. **Train** — methods, client norms, confidentiality and tooling before you touch live work.
3. **Deliver** — contribute on an active engagement under a project lead.
4. **Lead** — experienced consultants take phase ownership and mentor newer members.
